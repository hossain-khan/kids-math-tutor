# Privacy Policy for Kids Math Pup Tutor

**Last Updated: December 21, 2025**

## Introduction

Kids Math Pup Tutor ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children who use our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you or your child uses our mobile application.

This app is designed for children ages 5-8 (Kindergarten through Grade 2) and complies with:
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- Google Play Families Policy
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted to our servers:

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| Child's first name (optional) | Personalization of greetings | Device only |
| Grade level selection | Adjusting problem difficulty | Device only |
| Practice session history | Progress tracking | Device only |
| Game scores and times | Personal best tracking | Device only |
| Badge achievements | Motivation and rewards | Device only |
| App preferences | Customization settings | Device only |

**We do NOT have access to any of this locally stored data.**

### Analytics Data (Optional)

If analytics is enabled (it can be disabled in Settings), we collect **anonymized, aggregate data** through Firebase Analytics:

| Data Type | Purpose | Identifiable? |
|-----------|---------|---------------|
| Screen views | Understanding app usage | No |
| Feature usage | Improving app features | No |
| Session duration | App performance | No |
| Crash reports | Bug fixing | No |
| Device type | Compatibility testing | No |

**Analytics does NOT collect:**
- Names or personal information
- Location data
- Photos or media
- Contact information
- Any information that could identify your child

### How to Disable Analytics

Parents can disable analytics collection at any time:
1. Open the app
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Scroll to **Privacy** section
4. Toggle off **Analytics**

When disabled, no usage data is collected or transmitted.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we **never** collect:

- ❌ Full names or surnames
- ❌ Email addresses
- ❌ Phone numbers
- ❌ Physical addresses
- ❌ Photos or videos
- ❌ Location data (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
- ❌ Contact lists
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Social media accounts
- ❌ Payment information
- ❌ Device identifiers for advertising
- ❌ Any personally identifiable information (PII)

## How We Use Information

### Local Data Usage
- **Personalization**: Display the child's name in greetings (if provided)
- **Difficulty Adjustment**: Use grade level to show appropriate math problems
- **Progress Tracking**: Show practice history, streaks, and statistics
- **Motivation**: Award badges and track achievements
- **Preferences**: Remember audio, haptic, and display settings

### Analytics Data Usage (if enabled)
- **App Improvement**: Understand which features are used most
- **Bug Fixing**: Identify and resolve crashes
- **Performance**: Ensure the app runs smoothly on various devices

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All personal data is stored in encrypted local databases on your device
- Data is protected by your device's security features (PIN, fingerprint, etc.)
- We cannot access locally stored data

### Analytics Data (if enabled)
- Anonymized analytics data is processed by Firebase (Google)
- Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
- No personal identifiers are collected or stored
- See [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- Remains on your device until you delete it
- Delete all data: Settings → Clear App Data
- Uninstalling the app removes all local data

### Analytics Data
- Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months
- Data is aggregated and anonymized
- Cannot be traced back to individual users

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

1. **No Account Required**: Children can use the app without creating an account
2. **Minimal Data Collection**: We collect only what's necessary for app functionality
3. **Parental Control**: Parents can disable analytics at any time
4. **No Behavioral Advertising**: We do not serve ads or track for advertising
5. **No Social Features**: No chat, messaging, or social media integration
6. **No External Links**: No links to external websites or apps
7. **No In-App Purchases**: No ability to make purchases

## Third-Party Services

### Firebase Analytics (Google)
- **Purpose**: Anonymous usage analytics
- **Data Collected**: Non-personal usage statistics
- **Optional**: Can be disabled in Settings
- **Privacy Policy**: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Firebase Crashlytics (Google)
- **Purpose**: Crash reporting for bug fixes
- **Data Collected**: Device type, OS version, crash logs
- **No PII**: Does not collect personal information
- **Privacy Policy**: [firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

We do **not** use any advertising SDKs or networks.

## Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

1. **Review Data**: View all data stored in the app (Settings → Stats)
2. **Delete Data**: Clear all app data (Settings → Clear App Data)
3. **Disable Analytics**: Turn off data collection (Settings → Privacy)
4. **Contact Us**: Ask questions about our privacy practices

## Data Sharing

**We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information.**

We may share anonymized, aggregate analytics data only to:
- Improve our app's performance
- Fix bugs and crashes
- Understand feature usage

This data cannot identify any individual user.

- We comply with GDPR for European users
- You can disable analytics to prevent any data transfer
